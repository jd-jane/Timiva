Timiva respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we may handle data, optional analytics, cookies, browser local storage, third-party services, and contact information when you use Timiva’s website and tools.

Timiva is a free time and date tools website. We currently do not require users to create an account or sign in to use the tools on the website.

## 1. Whether We Require an Account

Timiva currently does not provide a user account system, and we do not require you to register or sign in to use the tools on the website.

This means Timiva does not ask you to provide an account name, password, member profile, payment information, or subscription information.

If Timiva adds sign-in, account management, or other personalized features in the future, we may update this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information You Enter Into Tools

Timiva’s tools may ask you to enter event names, dates, times, birthdays, target years, or other settings for calculation, display, or a better user experience.

Most tool input is mainly processed in your browser. Timiva does not store the information you enter into tools as member profile data, because Timiva currently does not provide a user account system.

Timiva does not sell event names, dates, birthdays, goals, or other content that you enter into our tools.

## 3. Browser Local Storage

Some tools may use browser local storage, such as LocalStorage, to remember your last event name, date, theme setting, or other preferences, so that you can continue more easily the next time you open the tool.

Timiva may also save your analytics consent choice in browser local storage so the website can remember whether you have allowed optional Google Analytics. This consent record includes your choice and a timestamp. It is separate from the content you enter into tools.

This type of data is usually stored in your browser or on your device, not in a Timiva member database. You can clear website data, cookies, or local storage through your browser settings. Clearing this data may reset your analytics consent preference.

## 4. Information We May Collect

When you use Timiva, we may process the following types of information:

- Information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, message content, or screenshots when you contact us by email.
- Basic technical information needed to operate the website, such as browser type, device type, operating system, language settings, error logs, or approximate region.
- Information generated by cookies, browser local storage, or similar technologies, including your analytics consent choice.
- If you choose **Allow analytics**, Google Analytics 4 may process usage information as described in Section 5.

Timiva does not intentionally ask you to provide sensitive personal information, and we recommend that you do not include unnecessary sensitive information in messages you send to us.

Timiva does not intentionally send your name, email address, or other directly identifying personal information to Google Analytics through the website tools.

## 5. Google Analytics, Cookies, and Third-Party Services

### Google Analytics 4

Timiva uses **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** to understand how the website and tools are used and to improve the product.

Google tag (gtag.js) is loaded **only after you choose Allow analytics** in Timiva’s consent banner or in **Analytics settings** in the website footer. If you have not made a choice yet, if you choose **Necessary only**, or if you later change your choice to **Necessary only**, Timiva does not load the Google tag and does not send analytics data to Google Analytics.

Your consent choice is stored in browser local storage as described in Section 3.

When analytics is allowed, GA4 may process information such as:

- Page views and navigation within Timiva
- Scroll events when approximately 90% of a page’s vertical depth becomes visible
- Clicks on links that lead from Timiva to another website
- Session statistics
- Approximate location derived from IP information; Google states that GA4 does not log or store IP addresses
- Browser type, device type, operating system, and similar technical information

Timiva configures Google Analytics 4 with:

- Google Signals turned off
- User-provided data collection turned off
- No use of Google Ads or ad personalization through this analytics setup

Analytics data is processed by Google under the Google Analytics terms and related Google privacy documentation. Timiva does not control how Google processes data once it is received.

In our Google Analytics 4 property settings, user-level and event-level data retention is set to **14 months**. Google’s standard aggregated reporting may be retained according to Google’s own service rules, which may differ from event-level retention.

If you withdraw consent, Timiva stops further analytics collection and attempts to clear `_ga*` cookies that the browser can access. Events already sent to Google Analytics before withdrawal cannot be automatically removed from Google’s systems through Timiva.

### Cookies and possible future advertising

Timiva currently uses browser local storage to remember tool preferences and analytics consent. When analytics is allowed, Google Analytics 4 may use cookies and similar technologies. Additional technologies may be introduced in the future if Timiva’s services change, and this policy may be updated accordingly.

Timiva **may in the future** display ads through third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense or similar services. **Timiva does not currently use third-party advertising for personalized ad tracking as part of the optional analytics setup described above.** If advertising is added in the future, this policy may be updated.

If third-party advertising is used in the future, those services may use cookies, browser local storage, device information, IP address, browser type, interaction records, or similar technologies to display ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, or control ad frequency.

How third-party services handle data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Timiva cannot fully control how third-party services use data collected through their technologies.

## 6. Consent Choices and Privacy Messages

Timiva shows a consent banner and **Analytics settings** in the website footer so you can choose whether to allow optional Google Analytics. You can change your choice at any time through **Analytics settings**.

These choices are saved in browser local storage as described in Section 3. Disabling or clearing site data may reset your preference.

If you are located in a region with specific requirements for cookies or analytics consent, you may use these Timiva controls to manage optional analytics. Additional legal requirements may apply depending on your location.

## 7. How We Use Information

Timiva may use information for the following purposes:

- To provide, maintain, and improve the website and tool features.
- To remember your tool settings, preferences, or analytics consent choice.
- To analyze website usage and improve the user experience when you allow analytics.
- To detect errors, maintain security, and prevent abuse.
- To respond to your emails, questions, bug reports, or feature suggestions.
- To comply with legal requirements, service policies, or third-party platform requirements.

If Timiva adds advertising in the future, related information may also be used to display, manage, or measure advertising, as described in an updated version of this policy.

## 8. How We Share Information

Timiva does not sell the content you enter into our tools.

We may process or share necessary information with third-party services in the following situations:

- To use website hosting, analytics, security, or error monitoring services.
- When you allow analytics, to use Google Analytics 4 as described in Section 5.
- To comply with laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful government requests.
- To protect the rights, safety, and property of Timiva, users, or the public.

If Timiva adds advertising in the future, we may also use third-party services that help display ads, measure ad performance, or prevent abuse, as described in an updated version of this policy.

Third-party services process data under their own policies, contractual terms, and applicable laws.

## 9. External Links

Timiva may include links to external websites, third-party ads, or other third-party content. When you click an external link or ad, you may leave Timiva. The content, privacy practices, and terms of external websites are not controlled by Timiva.

We recommend that you read the privacy policies and terms of third-party websites or services before using them.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

Timiva is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Timiva without appropriate consent, please contact us using the contact information below.

## 11. Data Retention

Timiva keeps necessary information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. For example, contact emails may be kept for a period of time so that we can handle issues, track bugs, or improve the service.

Data stored locally in your browser, such as LocalStorage, may remain on your device or in your browser until you clear browser data, website data, or use a clear function provided by the tool. This includes your analytics consent choice.

For Google Analytics 4, user-level and event-level data retention in our property settings is **14 months**, as described in Section 5. Google may retain certain aggregated reporting under its own service rules.

## 12. Your Choices and Controls

You can manage certain data and preferences in the following ways:

- Choose **Allow analytics** or **Necessary only** in Timiva’s consent banner or **Analytics settings** in the website footer.
- Block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
- Clear browser website data or LocalStorage.
- Use privacy settings provided by your device or browser.

Please note that disabling certain cookies or local storage may affect some tool features, your analytics consent preference, or the user experience.

If Timiva adds third-party advertising in the future, you may also be able to use ad preference tools provided by those services, as described in an updated version of this policy.

## 13. Updates to This Policy

Timiva may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website features, third-party services, analytics settings, possible future advertising, legal requirements, or how we operate.

The updated version will be posted on this page, and the last updated date at the top of the page will be changed. We recommend that you check this policy occasionally.
