Timiva respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we may handle data, cookies, browser local storage, third-party services, and contact information when you use Timiva’s website and tools.

Timiva is a free time and date tools website. We currently do not require users to create an account or sign in to use the tools on the website.

## 1. Whether We Require an Account

Timiva currently does not provide a user account system, and we do not require you to register or sign in to use the tools on the website.

This means Timiva does not ask you to provide an account name, password, member profile, payment information, or subscription information.

If Timiva adds sign-in, account management, or other personalized features in the future, we may update this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Information You Enter Into Tools

Timiva’s tools may ask you to enter event names, dates, times, birthdays, target years, or other settings for calculation, display, or a better user experience.

Most tool input is mainly processed in your browser. Timiva does not store the information you enter into tools as member profile data, because Timiva currently does not provide a user account system.

Timiva does not sell event names, dates, birthdays, goals, or other content that you enter into our tools.

## 3. Browser Local Storage

Some tools may use browser local storage, such as LocalStorage, to remember your last event name, date, theme setting, or other preferences, so that you can continue more easily the next time you open the tool.

This type of data is usually stored in your browser or on your device, not in a Timiva member database. You can clear website data, cookies, or local storage through your browser settings.

## 4. Information We May Collect

When you use Timiva, we may process the following types of information:

- Information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, message content, or screenshots when you contact us by email.
- Basic technical information, such as browser type, device type, operating system, language settings, page views, error logs, or approximate region.
- Information generated by cookies, browser local storage, or similar technologies.
- Anonymous or aggregated data generated by third-party analytics, advertising, or security services.

Timiva does not intentionally ask you to provide sensitive personal information, and we recommend that you do not include unnecessary sensitive information in messages you send to us.

## 5. Cookies, Advertising, and Third-Party Services

Timiva may use cookies, browser local storage, pixel tags, device identifiers, or similar technologies now or in the future to provide website features, maintain service stability, improve the user experience, analyze traffic, display ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, or control ad frequency.

Timiva may display ads through third-party advertising services now or in the future, such as Google AdSense or similar services. These third-party services may use cookies, browser local storage, device information, IP address, browser type, interaction records, or similar technologies to display personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, or control ad frequency.

Third-party ad providers, including Google, may show ads based on your visits to Timiva or other websites. You can manage cookies and personalized ad preferences through your browser settings, device settings, or ad preference tools provided by third-party advertising services.

How third-party services handle data is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Timiva cannot fully control how third-party services use data collected through their technologies.

## 6. Cookie Consent and Privacy Messages

Where required by law or by third-party service requirements, Timiva may show cookie consent banners, privacy messages, or consent management options to allow users in certain regions to manage cookies, ad personalization, or data processing preferences.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or other regions with specific requirements for cookies and advertising consent, Timiva may use a consent management platform or similar tool to process your consent choices in accordance with applicable laws or third-party advertising service requirements.

## 7. How We Use Information

Timiva may use information for the following purposes:

- To provide, maintain, and improve the website and tool features.
- To remember your tool settings or preferences.
- To analyze website usage and improve the user experience.
- To detect errors, maintain security, and prevent abuse.
- To display, manage, and measure advertising.
- To respond to your emails, questions, bug reports, or feature suggestions.
- To comply with legal requirements, service policies, or third-party platform requirements.

## 8. How We Share Information

Timiva does not sell the content you enter into our tools.

We may process or share necessary information with third-party services in the following situations:

- To use website hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or error monitoring services.
- To use third-party services that help display ads, measure ad performance, or prevent abuse.
- To comply with laws, regulations, court orders, or lawful government requests.
- To protect the rights, safety, and property of Timiva, users, or the public.

Third-party services may only process data according to their own policies, contracts, and applicable laws.

## 9. External Links

Timiva may include links to external websites, third-party ads, or other third-party content. When you click an external link or ad, you may leave Timiva. The content, privacy practices, and terms of external websites are not controlled by Timiva.

We recommend that you read the privacy policies and terms of third-party websites or services before using them.

## 10. Children’s Privacy

Timiva is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to Timiva without appropriate consent, please contact us using the contact information below.

## 11. Data Retention

Timiva keeps necessary information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. For example, contact emails may be kept for a period of time so that we can handle issues, track bugs, or improve the service.

Data stored locally in your browser, such as LocalStorage, may remain on your device or in your browser until you clear browser data, website data, or use a clear function provided by the tool.

## 12. Your Choices and Controls

You can manage certain data and preferences in the following ways:

- Block or delete cookies through your browser settings.
- Clear browser website data or LocalStorage.
- Use privacy settings provided by your device or browser.
- Use ad preference tools provided by third-party advertising services.
- Adjust cookie or advertising consent preferences when Timiva provides consent management options.

Please note that disabling certain cookies or local storage may affect some tool features or the user experience.

## 13. Updates to This Policy

Timiva may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website features, third-party services, advertising settings, legal requirements, or how we operate.

The updated version will be posted on this page, and the last updated date at the top of the page will be changed. We recommend that you check this policy occasionally.
