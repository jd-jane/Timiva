If you find an issue while using Timiva, or if you have an idea for a new time tool, you are welcome to contact us by email.

Timiva is a free time and date tools website. We currently do not provide user accounts, and you do not need to sign in to use the tools on the website.

## Contact Email

hello@timiva.app

## What You Can Contact Us About

You can email us about:

- Tool results that appear incorrect.
- Pages that do not display correctly on mobile, tablet, or desktop.
- Buttons, date pickers, sharing features, or other interactions that do not work.
- Ads that block tools, affect usability, or seem unsuitable for Timiva.
- New time or date tools you would like Timiva to add in the future.
- Other general questions related to the Timiva website.

## Information That Helps Us Understand the Issue

To help us understand the issue more quickly, you can include:

- The page URL where the issue happened.
- The device you are using, such as iPhone, Android, iPad, Mac, or Windows.
- The browser you are using, such as Safari, Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
- The steps you took before the issue happened.
- A screenshot or screen recording.
- Any error message or unexpected result you saw.

Please do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in your email, such as identity documents, payment information, passwords, medical information, or other private information.

## About Replies

We may read messages and evaluate whether a fix, content adjustment, or tool improvement is needed.

Because Timiva is a free tools website, we may not be able to reply to every email, and we cannot guarantee that every issue will be handled immediately. We will prioritize issues related to website stability, tool errors, mobile usability, or ads that block the tools.

## Advertising Issues

Timiva may display third-party ads now or in the future. If you see an ad that blocks a tool, affects usability, or seems unsuitable for Timiva, you can let us know by email.

Please include the page, device, browser, approximate region, and a screenshot if possible. This information can help us review the issue and adjust available ad settings.
